# 08 - Validated item banks for the 12 Axes²

Status: PARTIAL. Written 2026-10-31 by a research subagent. The web-search budget for this session ran out after about 25 searches and about 14 page fetches. Anything not checked against a primary document is marked **UNVERIFIED**. Exact item stems are given only where a primary PDF or page was read. Otherwise the entry gives the construct and source and says the wording still needs checking.

## 0. Bottom line

- **Verified in primary sources:** the 12-item SECS topic list and its 0-100 thermometer format (PLOS ONE); the ESS Round 8 module structure and citation rule; the WVS data-use conditions; the Pew 2016 enhancement items (worry and enthusiasm, and the "meddling with nature" item); the VSA's CC BY licence.
- **Partly verified (catalogue or abstract level):** Afrobarometer army-rule and presidential-dictatorship items; WVS Q245 (army takeover as a characteristic of democracy); ISSP National Identity and Role of Government topics; Pew 2014 trade items; the RFS-12 composition; the SDO7 structure.
- **Not verified:** most exact item stems for the psychometric scales (VSA, RFS-12, SDO7, Militaristic Attitudes Scale, SHAPE, TEQ), most ISSP and ESS item stems, and all WVS Wave 7 stems. These are listed as candidates only.
- **Licensing:** the strongest reuse position is the SECS (reported as CC0 in the paper) and the VSA (CC BY 3.0). WVS, ESS and ISSP permit citation-based use but do not state reuse terms for questionnaire wording. Pew and APA-published scales need a check before any verbatim reuse.
- **Axis coverage:** Democratic/Autocratic, Progressive/Traditional, Irreligious/Religious, and the economic axes have usable anchors. Federal/Unitary has no validated bank found, so it needs original items. Militarist/Pacifist, Internationalist/Nationalist, Globalist/Protectionist, Liberty/Security, Multicultural/Assimilationist and Technology/Bioconservative need original items or heavy adaptation.
- **Dual framing:** no source here validates an item under both "here and now (your country)" and "in my ideal system". Most WVS, ISSP and SECS items are either normative ("should") or present-tense, so a parallel item is needed for the other frame. Each axis below states which frame each anchor supports.

### 1.2 European Social Survey (ESS)

- **Structure (verified from the Round 8 source questionnaire, https://www.europeansocialsurvey.org/sites/default/files/2023-06/ESS8_source_questionnaires.pdf, pp. 1-2, read directly):**
  - Core B1-B43: politics, including political interest, trust, participation, party allegiance, socio-political orientations and immigration.
  - Core C1-C44: subjective wellbeing, social exclusion, crime, religion, perceived discrimination, national and ethnic identity, refugees.
  - Rotating D1-D32: climate change and energy.
  - Rotating E1-E42: welfare provision, claimant group sizes, attitudes to service delivery, likely future welfare dependence, EU referendum vote intention.
  - Core F: socio-demographics. Section H: Schwartz human values scale.
- **Citation and disclaimer (verified, same PDF p. 1).** "Please cite as follows: European Social Survey (2016). ESS Round 8 Source Questionnaire. London: ESS ERIC Headquarters c/o City University London." Also a disclaimer that ESS ERIC and its core team are not liable for uses of the information.

### 1.4 Pew Research Center

- **Human enhancement 2016 (verified from the report PDF, https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2016/07/PS_2016.07.26_Human-Enhancement-Survey_FINAL.pdf, pp. 3-5, read directly):**
  - Enthusiastic vs worried about gene editing for much reduced disease risk: 49% enthusiastic, 68% worried.
  - Brain chip implant for much improved cognitive abilities: 34% enthusiastic, 69% worried.
  - Synthetic blood for much improved physical abilities: 36% enthusiastic, 63% worried.
  - Would want enhancements to their own brains and blood: 32% and 35%. Would not want them: 66% and 63%.
  - Dividing question (chart label, verbatim): "[X] is meddling with nature and crosses a line we should not cross." Opposed frame, from the footnote: "as humans, we are always trying to better ourselves and this idea is no different."
  - Religious commitment index (footnote, verbatim concepts): high commitment means attending worship at least weekly, praying at least once a day, and saying religion is very important in one's life.
  - Survey: March 2-28, 2016, US adults (n = 4,726 per the search snippet).
- **Trade 2014 (partly verified).** Pew Global Attitudes Spring 2014, 44 countries, Q27-Q32 on trade and foreign investment; median 81% say trade and business ties are good for their country. Report: https://www.pewresearch.org/global/2014/09/16/global-views-of-trade/markdown (search snippet). Wording of the 2014 items: **UNVERIFIED** (a 2011 variant asked whether "growing trade and business ties" were good or bad for one's country, search snippet). Wording effects (mentioning jobs makes answers more hostile) are a secondary claim, **UNVERIFIED**.
- **Political Typology 2021.** Survey of 10,221 US adults, July 8-18, 2021, on the American Trends Panel. Questions and responses are published. A search snippet also says a revised typology was published in June 2026 (**UNVERIFIED**). Reuse terms for Pew content: **UNVERIFIED** (check pewresearch.org terms).

### 1.5 Psychometric scales

- **SECS, Everett 2013 (verified).** Source: https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0082131 (fetched).
  - 12 items, labelled by topic: Abortion; Welfare benefits (reverse scored); Limited government; Military and national security; Religion; Gun ownership; Traditional marriage; Traditional values; Fiscal responsibility; Business; The family unit; Patriotism.
  - Response format: 0-100 feeling thermometer in steps of 10 (0 = very negative, 100 = very positive). The 1-7 and 1-9 formats in the article apply to a single-item measure.
  - Reliability: full 12-item alpha .88; social subscale (7 items) .87; economic subscale (5 items) .70.
  - Samples: 41 MTurk participants for item selection, 291 analysed for validation (US). Not cross-national.
  - Subscale membership of each item is in the article's Table 4 and Materials S1, which I did not read. The social/economic split is **UNVERIFIED** per item.
  - Licence as reported by the fetched text: CC0 public domain dedication. Check the live page before relying on it.
  - Caveat: the item stems on the thermometer are topic labels. The exact question wording is in Materials S1 (**UNVERIFIED**).
- **Bizumic & Duckitt 2018, Very Short Authoritarianism (VSA).** Six items; two items for each of three subdimensions (authoritarian submission, conventionalism, authoritarian aggression); balanced positive and negative wording. Source: https://jspp.psychopen.eu/index.php/jspp/article/view/5047 (fetched; DOI 10.5964/jspp.v6i1.835). Licence: CC BY 3.0 (verified on the article page). Item wording: **UNVERIFIED** (the page excerpt lacked the stems). Reliability: the authors report alpha = .71; a later critique cites a range of .54-.66 (secondary, **UNVERIFIED**).
- **RWA short forms.** A search did not find a six-item RWA scale attributed to Zakrisson, Funke and Oesterreich. Zakrisson (2005) is a 15-item Swedish scale; Funke (2005) is a 12-item three-dimensional authoritarianism scale. Both **UNVERIFIED** for stems and reliability.
- **Revised Religious Fundamentalism Scale (RFS-12).** Altemeyer & Hunsberger 2004, *International Journal for the Psychology of Religion*, 14(1), 47-54. Twelve items; four original items kept, five revised, three added; half negatively worded; authors report reliability at least as high as the 20-item original. Source: https://relbib.de/Record/1846844878/Details (search snippet). Stems and licence: **UNVERIFIED**. A later Italian adaptation used it "with Altemeyer's permission", so permission is likely needed for the instrument.
- **SDO7, Ho et al. 2015.** 16 items; two subdimensions, SDO-Dominance and SDO-Egalitarianism (opposition to equality); short form 8 items. Journal of Personality and Social Psychology, 109(6), 1003-1028, DOI 10.1037/pspi0000033. APA supplementary materials include item files, e.g. https://supp.apa.org/psycarticles/supplemental/pspi0000033/z2g010154395so1.doc (URL seen in search, **UNVERIFIED** contents). The APA supplements are copyrighted; treat as citation-only unless licence checked. Note: seven of eight SDO-E items use "we" or "our" wording, and a follow-up showed this did not change criterion relationships (APA supplement snippet, **UNVERIFIED**).
- **Moral Foundations Questionnaire (MFQ-30).** Six items per foundation (care, fairness, loyalty, authority, sanctity) and two catch items. Credited to Graham, Haidt and Nosek (2008). A third-party self-scorable copy is at https://web.lemoyne.edu/~hevern/psy444_24F/documents/MFQ30.self-scorable.pdf. Licence: **UNVERIFIED**, check moralfoundations.org before use.
- **Militaristic Attitudes Scale (Özdemir & Sakallı-Uğurlu 2018).** Five factors: existence of the military, value of the military, militaristic system, political position of the military, compulsory military service. Alphas .75-.95. Developed on university students in Turkey (N = 339 and 583). Journal: *Peace and Conflict: Journal of Peace Psychology*, DOI 10.1037/pac0000296. Items and licence: **UNVERIFIED**. Source found: https://avesis.metu.edu.tr/yayin/35cf3674-b951-49ff-88b5-79e1c3cf24a5/development-of-militaristic-attitudes-scale-and-its-associations-with-turkish-identity-and-uninational-ideology (search snippet).
- **Nationalism vs patriotism.** Blank & Schmidt 2003, *Political Psychology* 24(2), 289-312, DOI 10.1111/0162-895X.00329. Kosterman & Feshbach 1989, *Political Psychology* 10, 257-274. Blank & Schmidt's nationalism items reportedly include three items for positive evaluation of collective goods (**UNVERIFIED**, from a 1997 German-language paper). Items: **UNVERIFIED**. A 2020s ISSP measurement-equivalence paper tests nationalism and constructive patriotism across 34 countries (Political Analysis, Cambridge; title seen in search).
- **Human enhancement and biotech attitudes.**
  - SHAPE scale (Villa et al. 2023, ACM IMWUT): 13 items measuring perceptions of users of augmentation technology, not general bioconservatism. Items: **UNVERIFIED**. https://research.aalto.fi/en/publications/societys-attitudes-towards-human-augmentation-and-performance-enh/ (search snippet).
  - Technological Enhancements Questionnaire (Stefański & Jach 2021): one-dimensional, pilot N = 139 young Polish adults, leans pro-enhancement. Items: **UNVERIFIED**.
  - Australian biotechnology attitudes study (2020): general and specific attitudes, exploratory factor analysis on two samples including a national sample. Items: **UNVERIFIED**.

## 4. Recommendation

1. **Well served by validated anchors** (after verifying stems): Democratic/Autocratic (Afrobarometer, WVS democracy items), Irreligious/Religious (Pew religious-commitment index, RFS-12), Progressive/Traditional (SECS social subscale, VSA conventionalism), Collectivize/Privatize and Planned/Markets (ISSP RoG, SECS economic subscale).
2. **Partly served; needs original twin items:** Multicultural/Assimilationist (ISSP NI, ESS immigration), Internationalist/Nationalist (ISSP NI, Blank & Schmidt constructs), Liberty/Security (ISSP RoG topics), Globalist/Protectionist (Pew 2014 trade), Technology/Bioconservative (Pew 2016 descriptive items).
3. **Needs original items:** Federal/Unitary (no bank found), Militarist/Pacifist (MAS is Turkish-student-based and needs cross-cultural validation).
4. **Dual-frame design rule:** for every anchor, write a "here and now" and an "ideal" version. Pilot both. Report the correlation between frames and any framing effects. No source here validates the two frames.
5. **Psychometric caution:** the SECS was built on a US MTurk sample of 291 with a 41-person item-selection pilot. Treat its subscales as a starting point, not a validated cross-national instrument.
6. **Next steps (not done):** (a) read the SECS Materials S1 for stems and subscale membership; (b) read the VSA full text for the six stems; (c) get WVS Wave 7 and ESS Round 8/11 questionnaires as text (not binary PDF) for exact wording; (d) request permissions from WVS Association and ESS ERIC; (e) search for federalism and multicultural-ideology scales (not covered); (f) recheck the Pew 2026 typology and ISSP licence claims.
