IDEAL SYSTEM frame (verbatim respondent text): "Picture your ideal system, already up and running and legitimate by your own lights. Its people are ordinary, fallible humans — including whoever holds power, whether that's assemblies, councils, a monarch, or no one. It still meets hard situations: downturns, conflict, crime, disruptive change. Other countries are as they realistically could be. In your ideal system, how much do you agree?"
HERE AND NOW frame — for archetype coding the reference context is FIXED: "a present-day developed liberal democracy (think a typical Western European country today), with its real institutions, people and problems. Here and now, how much do you agree?" Answer as an adherent of this ideology living there now would answer, given their ideology's transitional/practical programme (what they would actually support doing there now).
Shared rule: "If a statement describes a situation, assume it's happening in the frame you're judging." Scale: -3 strongly disagree, -2 disagree, -1 slightly disagree, 0 neutral/torn, +1 slightly agree, +2 agree, +3 strongly agree. Use x for pass ONLY if the ideology genuinely has no position on the statement (rare; prefer 0 when adherents are divided).
